# Job Targets — Remote or Jacksonville, FL (Pre-Degree)

Realistic near-term roles you can pursue now, using the bot as portfolio evidence, before finishing UF/Tsinghua. Titles/categories, not guaranteed openings — search these titles on LinkedIn/company career pages/AngelList-Wellfound directly; do not treat any specific company name here as a confirmed opening.

## Categories most winnable pre-degree (in priority order)

1. **Junior/Associate Trading Systems or Execution Engineer (remote-friendly)** — prop trading firms and smaller systematic funds hire off demonstrated engineering more than credentials. Your execution risk-gating (liquidity gate, notional ceilings) and the trade ledger/reconciliation work are directly relevant. Search terms: "trading systems engineer," "execution engineer," "low latency engineer junior."

2. **Quant/Data Engineer, market data or alt-data pipelines (remote)** — your multi-source news fallback (Alpaca→NewsAPI→Polygon→RSS), SEC EDGAR scraping, and retry/backoff reliability work is exactly this job's day-to-day. Alt-data vendors and hedge fund data-eng teams hire junior people who've actually built ingestion pipelines. Search terms: "data engineer alternative data," "market data engineer," "ETL engineer fintech."

3. **DevOps/SRE for a trading or fintech shop (remote)** — your docker-compose incident response (silent crash → persistent logging → verified recovery) is a legitimate SRE story. Fintech/trading shops value people who've operated infra under real failure conditions. Search terms: "site reliability engineer fintech," "platform engineer trading."

4. **Backtesting/research engineer (remote, junior)** — once you complete the tearsheet work in [[02_quant_evaluation_criteria]], this becomes a stronger target — building and maintaining backtest infrastructure (not generating alpha) is a hireable, less credential-gated role than "quant researcher."

5. **General software engineer roles in Jacksonville, FL specifically** — Jacksonville has a real fintech/insurance-tech cluster (large financial services back-office presence, e.g. major banks' regional operations, insurance companies, and a growing tech scene) even without dedicated quant trading shops locally. Realistic local targets: backend/software engineer roles at regional banks' technology divisions, insurance-tech companies, or fintech-adjacent startups — use the bot as your systems/backend portfolio piece even if the role isn't trading-specific. Search terms: "software engineer Jacksonville FL," "backend engineer fintech Jacksonville."

## How to position yourself for each
- Lead with the incident-response story and the risk-gating engineering for SRE/execution-engineer roles — these hiring managers care about "has this person operated something that broke."
- Lead with the data pipeline reliability work (retry/backoff, refuse-on-incomplete-data) for data-engineer roles.
- Lead with the backtest/tearsheet work (once built) for research-engineer roles — don't apply to these until you have real out-of-sample numbers, or you'll get exposed in the first technical question.
- For all of these: be upfront that you're pre-degree, currently completing UF prereqs. Frame it as "actively finishing formal credentials while already shipping production systems" — a strength, not something to downplay, especially for engineering-heavy (not research-heavy) roles where degree-gating is weaker.
